    About

    Wildlife records from the Queensland WildNet Data API and the Atlas of Living Australia. Reference photos via ALA. Map by OpenTopoMap and OpenStreetMap contributors. Zones are built from where animals have been recorded; no exact locations are ever shown. Your sightings, photos and badges stay on this device. Friend links share only counts and badges.

    DECO1800 team project, Hairy Penguins.
